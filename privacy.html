Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 August 2026

Anchor is a personal time-blocking app. This policy explains what data Anchor handles and - more importantly - what it doesn't.

The short version

Anchor does not collect your data. Everything you create in Anchor lives on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your personal iCloud account. There are no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no access to anything you do in the app.

Calendar and Reminders access

Anchor asks for full access to your Apple Calendar and Apple Reminders because its core purpose is to organise your existing reminders into time blocks scheduled around your calendar events. With this access, Anchor:

  • reads your calendar events to schedule time blocks around them, and updates events if you choose to do this in Anchor;
  • reads your reminders to show the relevant ones during each time block, and creates, edits, and completes reminders when you do so in the app.

All of this happens on your device through Apple's EventKit framework. Your calendar and reminder content is never transmitted off your device to me or to any third party. Anchor does not read or modify reminder alarms.

Data Anchor stores

Anchor keeps its own data - time blocks, templates, tags, session history, and settings - in a local database on your device. This data never leaves your device except through iCloud sync (below) or an export you initiate yourself.

iCloud sync

If you're signed into iCloud, Anchor syncs its data between your devices using your private iCloud (CloudKit) container. This container belongs to your Apple ID: Apple hosts it, only your devices can read it, and I cannot access, view, or retrieve anything in it. Turning off iCloud for Anchor in your device settings stops sync; Anchor continues to work locally.

Purchases

Anchor's subscription is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see or store your payment details. Apple provides me only with anonymous transaction records (for example, that a subscription was purchased), never your identity.

Diagnostics

If you have opted in to sharing analytics with app developers in your device's Privacy settings, Apple may send me anonymised crash reports and performance metrics. These contain technical information about the crash, not your content, and are used solely to fix bugs. If you haven't opted in, nothing is received.

What Anchor does not do

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising
  • No accounts — there is nothing to sign up for
  • No selling, sharing, or transferring of data to anyone
  • No data collection as defined by Apple's privacy guidelines: Anchor's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected"

Retention and deletion

Your data stays as long as you keep it. To remove it: delete anything within the app, or delete the app itself to remove all local data. To remove synced data from iCloud, delete the app's iCloud data via Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, or turn off iCloud for Anchor. Reminders and calendar events belong to Apple's apps and remain in Reminders and Calendar unless you delete them there.

Children

Anchor is a productivity tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If Anchor's data practices ever change, this policy will be updated and the effective date revised. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
